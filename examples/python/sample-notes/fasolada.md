# Fasolada (bean soup)

Soak 500 g of dried white beans overnight. Drain and rinse.

Cook the beans with one chopped onion, two carrots, two celery sticks, a
spoon of tomato paste and a bay leaf, covered with water, for about 90 minutes.

Add a generous glass of olive oil in the last 20 minutes. Season with salt only
at the end, or the beans stay hard. Serves six.
